% --- Slide 4: The idea ---
\begin{frame}{The idea: filter LLM samples in embedding space}
\begin{columns}[T]
\begin{column}{0.54\textwidth}
\small
LLM samples have \textbf{heterogeneous quality}: some land inside their class region,
others in peripheral or cross-class zones. Adding all of them can \alert{hurt} the classifier.

\vspace{0.5em}
\textbf{Post-generation geometric filtering:}
\begin{enumerate}\setlength\itemsep{1pt}\footnotesize
  \item Generate a \textbf{3$\times$ surplus} of candidates with an LLM.
  \item Embed them with the same sentence encoder.
  \item Score each candidate by its \textbf{distance to real examples} of its class.
  \item Keep the best; \textbf{weight} them by their score.
\end{enumerate}

\vspace{0.4em}
\begin{insight}
\footnotesize
\textbf{Research question:} can geometric filtering make LLM augmentation
reliably better than SMOTE in few-shot settings?
\end{insight}
\end{column}
