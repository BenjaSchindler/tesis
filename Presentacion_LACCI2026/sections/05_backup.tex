\begin{insight}
\footnotesize
Most of the contribution comes from \textbf{geometric selection itself} ($+1.75$ pp over no augmentation,
$p<0.0001$); soft weighting adds a small, marginally significant refinement ($+0.22$ pp).
\end{insight}

\vspace{0.4em}
\scriptsize
Binary filter vs.\ SMOTE: $+2.39$ pp, $d=0.82$.\\
No augmentation already beats SMOTE by $+0.64$ pp.

\vspace{0.4em}
\footnotesize
\textbf{Caching caveat:} cached LLM responses give zero cross-seed variance for deterministic
classifiers (80\% of configs). Restricted to stochastic classifiers the effect remains:
$+1.79$ pp, $p=0.0015$, $d=0.52$.
\end{column}
